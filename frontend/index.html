5 · Export

Downloads stay on this device. Video records the stage while the sequence plays, muxing the word audio (Reactor's captureStream + MediaRecorder pattern, MP4 first for iOS Safari). The grade is a shader pass; the GLB download carries the model and its morph animation as loaded.

What each format carries, exactly. GLB: the model, its morph targets, and the weights animation. STL: static geometry only, for fabrication and CAD; no morph targets, no animation, no color or material, and no grade. USDZ: one static neutral pose for AR preview, built by the local backend; no morph targets or animation (Quick Look drops them from USDZ), and no grade.
